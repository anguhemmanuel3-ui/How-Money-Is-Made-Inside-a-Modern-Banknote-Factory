### Main Section 1 — The basic concept
What is anti-counterfeiting technology? It is a broad category covering security features, materials, printing technologies, authentication methods and production controls designed to make unauthorized reproduction or alteration more difficult and detectable.

### Main Section 2 — Where it fits
A modern security-printing environment is made up of multiple connected stages. Depending on the authorized application, these can include substrate handling, printing, security processes, inspection, identification or numbering, finishing and secure handling.

A key point is that **one machine does not represent an entire banknote-production system**. Production equipment is selected and integrated around the requirements of the authorized facility.

### Main Section 3 — Quality and security
Quality control is a major part of professional security printing. Inspection systems and controlled production processes help organizations maintain consistency and identify production issues.

Security printing also depends on the combination of materials, technology, controlled access, traceability and authentication—not simply the appearance of the printed product.
